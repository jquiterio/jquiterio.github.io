Principles

How I try to lead

  1. Trace every decision to an objectiveA standard, a control or a reorganisation should be explainable in terms of what the organisation is trying to achieve. If it cannot be, it is probably overhead.
  2. Standardise the routine, spend attention on the hard partsShared tools and conventions remove decisions nobody needs to make twice, and free engineers for the problems that are specific to their system.
  3. Facilitate decisions rather than hoard themSecurity and platform teams do their best work when they give risk owners clear options and consequences, and let the owners decide.
  4. Write it downDistributed teams across time zones run on written decisions, written context and written reasons. Meetings are for what writing cannot settle.
  5. Measure what the strategy needsA few measures that reflect strategic priorities are worth more than a dashboard of everything that is easy to count.
Open questions

Questions I am working through

  1. How do you lead specialists who know more than you do in their own field?
  2. How light can governance be and still be followed?
  3. What does a technical leader owe the team when the organisation changes around it?
  4. How should performance be measured in work whose value is mostly risk avoided?
