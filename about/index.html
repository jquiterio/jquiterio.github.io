Biography

I work in information security for European space infrastructure at SES in Luxembourg. Before that I spent twenty years in software: Linux and network engineering in Portugal and Angola, an open-source services company of my own, DevOps consulting for European organisations, and several years building and leading platform and DevSecOps teams for satellite software.

Alongside that work I am a doctoral candidate in Applied Mathematics & Modelling at Universidade Aberta, where my research concerns autoregressive models with non-normal errors. I also hold a master's diploma in Open Source Software from ISCTE, completed MIT's programme in Management of Technology, and am studying for an MBA at the Luxembourg School of Business.

I am Portuguese and have lived and worked in several European countries. I write in English here, and work across several of Europe's languages.

The views on this site are my own and do not represent any employer or institution.
